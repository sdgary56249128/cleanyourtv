# Personal Chromecast space wallpapers

This fork-only preset keeps the four-app dock and YouTube-to-SmartTube mapping, and rotates three generated science-fiction landscapes every 30 minutes. Images are 1920×1080 JPEGs under 300 KB. `wallpaper_true_color` keeps dark sky gradients smooth on the Chromecast 4K with 2 GB RAM.

Apply from the repository root after setting `TV_ADB_BINARY` and `TV_HOST` to the paired connection:

```bash
tools/theme.sh personal/chromecast-space
```

The wallpapers were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool (not an API/CLI workflow), then exported to JPEG. They are imaginative artwork, not astronomical photography. No third-party image service is required.

## Assets and final prompts

### [ringed-lake](wallpapers/01-ringed-lake.jpg)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic sci-fi space landscape wallpaper for a television launcher. Primary request: A remote alpine lake on an alien moon, a huge softly lit ringed planet rising in the right half of the sky, tiny distant moons, rich sparse star field reflected in still water, dark silhouetted mountain ridges. Style: refined realistic digital matte painting, premium cinematic science-fiction environment, tranquil and atmospheric. Composition: full-bleed horizontal 16:9, ideally 1920x1080 or higher; upper-left 35 percent stays very dark, low-detail negative space for a clock; bottom 38 percent dark and uncluttered for app tiles. Keep the main celestial detail in upper-right and middle distance. Lighting: controlled highlights, smooth natural gradients, deep navy blacks, subtle luminous color, never a bright white sky. No people, no text, no letters, no UI, no borders, no logos, no watermark. One image only.
